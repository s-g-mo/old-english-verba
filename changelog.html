A running record of additions and changes to the site, most recent first.

3 Jun 2026
Add verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 5 & 6
  • Added 17 verbs from Chapters 5 and 6, including gān, dōn, onġietan, onġinnan, ġesċieppan, nytan, and others
  • Fixed strong verb class inference for prefixed verbs (on-, onġ-) and ie-stem Class VI verbs
31 May 2026
Conjugation accuracy improvements
  • Fixed a number of incorrect verb forms across strong and weak verb classes, identified by systematic comparison against Wiktionary and the Ōsweald Bera workbook
  • Fixed many of the collision rules in the verb conjugation engine
29 May 2026
Rule-based conjugation engine and new Rules page
  • Added a Rules page explaining how Old English verb forms are mechanically derived from principal parts, with worked examples and syntax-highlighted code snippets in Python and JavaScript
  • Verb conjugation tables are now generated programmatically at runtime from a small set of inputs, rather than stored manually — strong verbs need only their four principal parts, weak verbs only their infinitive
  • Verb class (Strong IV, Weak 1 etc.) is now inferred automatically from the ablaut pattern rather than stored explicitly
  • Renamed Grammar page (formerly Basics)
  • Syntax highlighting added to all code blocks on the Rules page
  • Added verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 2, 3, & 4

  • Added sources tags on verb cards

28 May 2026
Initial site setup and starter verbs
  • Five-page structure: landing page, verb conjugation reference, grammar basics reference, about page, and changelog
  • 12 starter verbs covering Strong classes I–VII, Weak classes I–II, bēon (irregular, with dual eom- and bēo- paradigms), and wītan (preterite-present)
  • Full conjugation display: present and past indicative, present and past subjunctive, non-finite forms, and imperative
  • Live search filtering the verb sidebar by Old English headword or English gloss
