A running record of additions and changes to the site, most recent first.

23 Jul 2026
Related tags now cover whole prefix families
  • Related tags on verb cards are now derived automatically: a verb and all its prefixed variants link to each other, with the base verb listed first (e.g. sittan ↔ besittan, forsittan, ġesittan) — previously only a handful of hand-tagged links existed
  • 52 verb families across the corpus, including families whose base verb isn't on the site (beġietan / forġietan / onġietan; onfōn / befōn / underfōn)
  • Look-alike but unrelated verbs stay apart (āgan vs āgān, reċċan vs rēċan), with regression tests pinning every family
23 Jul 2026
Full conjugation audit against Wiktionary, and fixes
  • Audited all 330 verbs form-by-form against their Wiktionary conjugation tables — over 98% of forms matched outright or differed only by deliberate site conventions
  • Fixed 19 verbs with genuine errors: ġe- prefixed verbs whose 2nd/3rd person present corrupted the prefix (ġesitst, not ġisitst), six missing epenthetic imperatives (bide, liġe, ġesite, and others), erian reclassified as weak 1, and corrected past or participle forms for grētan, tellan, beġietan, and wadan
  • Fixed diacritics on slǣpan, ċīdan, onġietan (past onġeat), eġlan, and libban's past participle
23 Jul 2026
New verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 26 & 27, and an engine fix
  • Added 14 verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 26 and 27, including ondrǣdan, āhebban, wiþsacan, forġieldan, ālīesan, ġehȳdan, and others
  • Fixed the weak 1 past tense of stems ending in a single s, which now correctly take -de (ālīesan → ālīesde, not ālīeste)
  • Added missing chapter tags for drīfan (Chapter 9) and hīeran (Chapter 8)
14 Jul 2026
Study Mode (v0.1) — flashcards and drilling practice
  • New Study page: quiz yourself on verb meanings (gloss ↔ lemma) and conjugated forms, filtered by chapter, verb type, and which grammatical forms to include
  • Multiple-choice (with A–D keyboard shortcuts) and fill-in-the-blank modes, run separately or mixed within a round
  • Grading is macron-insensitive and accepts "th" for þ/ð and "ae" for æ; gloss answers accept any listed sense (e.g. "fear" or "be afraid" for a gloss of "to be afraid, fear")
  • Results screen lists missed cards for review, plus a locally-saved best score per filter combination
  • Known limitation: a handful of verb pairs with overlapping meanings (e.g. ċēosan/ġeċēosan, both roughly "to choose") can mark an equally valid answer wrong — planned for a future pass
13 Jul 2026
New verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 21–25, and engine fixes
  • Added 61 verbs from Ōsweald Bera Chapters 21 through 25, including iernan, tēon, ġebiddan, oþstandan, ġemunan, āgān, styrian, bewerian, and others
  • Fixed prefixed weak verbs incorrectly receiving an additional ġe- on their past participles — corrects 11 verbs including āflīeman, āsettan, forbærnan, ācwellan, and ūtian
  • Fixed styrian and bewerian misclassified as Weak II — both are light-stem -rian verbs like ġebyrian and needed the same manual treatment
  • Fixed tēon missing its contracted present tense (tīehþ, not the regular-pattern tīenþ), matching the already-correct ātēon
  • Fixed several dental-assimilation and consonant-gemination irregularities in new verbs' past tense and past participles (ġeswenċan, ġemētan, ġesǣlan, ġestillan, mǣtan, ċierran, ēhtan, ōleċċan)
  • Fixed ġebiddan's 2nd/3rd person present indicative, where i-mutation was corrupting the ġe- prefix itself (ġibitst) instead of mutating only the root vowel (ġebitst)
  • Fixed missing macrons on berēafian and ġesǣlan
  • Added a classOverride field for verbs whose class can't be inferred automatically, such as double-prefix compounds like forþġewītan, and a new Class IIIa ablaut row for the iernan-type breaking pattern
