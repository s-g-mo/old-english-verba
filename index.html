Old English Verbs

A Bescherelle-style verb conjugation reference
for students of Old English.

Old English verb conjugation is notoriously difficult to internalize. Modelled on the French Bescherelle tradition, this site provides a clean, structured reference for every inflected verb form, organized by verb class. It began as a personal study tool and is growing from there.
